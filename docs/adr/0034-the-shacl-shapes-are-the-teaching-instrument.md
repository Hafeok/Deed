# 0034. The SHACL shapes are the teaching instrument

**Status:** Accepted by Emil before 2026-11-15, as one of the decisions the project started from. Recorded 2026-11-16 (issue #2).

**Context.** A validator that reports malformed data teaches nothing about design.

**Decision.** The shapes' messages are written as design guidance. Each names the question the author has not answered.

**Consequences.**
- Using Deed shows what a well-designed act entails.
- Messages are part of the product and are tested (issue #36).

**Alternatives considered.**
- Terse technical messages with separate documentation. Rejected: the guidance would not be where the author is.
